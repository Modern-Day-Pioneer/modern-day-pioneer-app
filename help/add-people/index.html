Add people, pets and families

  1. Go to People and click Add an individual.
  2. Type the name the family uses, choose Person or Pet, and click Add.
  3. Fill in what you know. Dates can be rough, like “about 1905”. Add a few words about them, then click Save changes.

  4. Under Relationships, say how they are connected: start typing a name and pick it from the list. The app suggests relationships that follow, like grandparents; click Yes, add it or Not right.

  5. Under Events, add the moments of their life: choose the Kind of event (birth, wedding, move and so on), give a date if you know it, and click Add event. If the kind you need isn’t listed, choose Add a new kind… and type it, such as “Mission” or “Baptism”; it’s then offered for everyone in the Journey.

Families: on People, click New family, give it a name such as “The Hales”, and add its members. Someone can belong to more than one family.

Lost someone? Make an In memory of page about them.
