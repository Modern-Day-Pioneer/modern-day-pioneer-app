Bring in photos

Your originals are never changed: the app copies them into the Journey.

A few files

  1. On Home or Waiting, click Add photos and recordings, then Choose files.
  2. Pick the photos and click Open. They land in Waiting, a holding place for pictures and recordings that aren’t in a memory yet. Show as changes how Waiting looks: Tiles, List, By month or By box (one box per month or per import).

A whole folder

  1. Click Add photos and recordings, then Choose a folder (with a preview)… (in older versions, Bring in a folder with a preview…), or drag the folder onto the app’s window.
  2. Pick the folder. You see a preview first, one row per file: the Preview column shows each picture small and lets you play recordings and videos, dates are read from folder and file names, and names of people already in the Journey are suggested. Untick anything you don’t want.
  3. Click Bring in. Undo takes the whole import back.

Folders sorted by family (import rules) If your pictures are already sorted into folders, one per person or couple, with folders for eras, days and events inside, choose Family history folders (built in) under Import rules in the preview. The rules fill in each file’s memory, story, family, date, place and people from the folder and file names, and the list under the rules shows what each rule caught and any files no rule caught. To change the rules, click Save the built-in rules as a file…, edit the file in any text editor, and save it as _import-rules.md at the top of your folders: the app then uses it for that folder every time. The file also holds lists of first names, initials, spelling fixes and folders to skip. It stays on your computer.

Got a big pile? Suggested groups turns it into memories quickly.

Google Photos

  1. Click Add photos and recordings, then Bring in from Google Photos, and follow the three steps: ask Google Takeout for a copy, download every part into one folder without unzipping, then Choose the zip files.
  2. Photos come in with their dates, captions and the people tagged in them.
