Make a story

A story gathers memories around one event, person or group, and becomes one book on the shelf of the exported history. A memory can be in more than one story.

  1. Go to Stories & Memories and click New story. Give it a title, like “Life with Walter”, and click Add.
  2. If the story is about someone, choose them. Write a short introduction, then Save changes.
  3. Click Add memories already in the Journey, tick the ones that belong, and click Add.
  4. Use Move up and Move down to put them in order. The cover is the first picture in the first memory that has one.

You can also start a new memory right inside the story, or bring in photos from Waiting.
