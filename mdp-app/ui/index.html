                  Bring in a folder

                  Choose a folder of pictures and recordings that is already sorted. The app guesses each file's date, title, people and place from the folder and file names; check them, fix anything, then bring the files in. Zip files are read as they are, without unzipping: choose the zips themselves (for a Google Takeout, all its parts at once), or a folder holding them. A history this app exported can be brought back in the same way, which also joins two Journeys together. Your originals are copied, never changed.

                              Back up and restore

                              A backup is one .zip file holding the whole Journey: every picture, recording and note, and the catalog. Keep it on a USB drive or another computer. It opens with any zip tool, and Restore puts it back as a Journey.

                              Bring in from Google Photos

                              1. Ask Google for a copy of your photos. The Google Takeout page opens with only Google Photos selected. Keep .zip files as the file type.
                              2. When Google emails you, download every part into one folder. Don't unzip them.
                              3. Choose all the zip files. The app reads them where they are, pairs each photo with its dates and places, and shows a preview before anything comes in.

                              Already unzipped them? Choose "Bring in a folder with a preview…" and pick the folder that holds the parts.
