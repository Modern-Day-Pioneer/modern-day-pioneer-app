Scrapbook look props (sprint 16).

AI-made with Comfy Cloud (Qwen-Image text-to-image) from text prompts; the untouched originals,
prompts and seeds are kept with the project's design files. Covered by the project's AI disclosure.
These are WebP copies shrunk to twice their size on screen. Decoration only: CSS backgrounds that
carry no words or meaning, shown only in the Scrapbook look and never in high contrast. Lit from
the top left, so one set serves the light oak and the walnut desk.

Size aim: props and textures at most 1 MB in the app. These six come to about 45 KB.

Sprint 17: waiting-box.webp (about 58 KB), an open cardboard archive box seen from the front and
above, made the same way (seed 90211). Its floor and the background around it are cut out, keeping
the shadows as soft black, so Waiting's List and Tiles scroll inside it. Stretched by border-image
slices (136 159 156 99), so it fits any window.

Sprint 17 (Jared, 2026-11-05): notepad.webp and pushpin.webp were dropped. "Memories to finish" is
a legal pad drawn in CSS (flat leatherette binding, yellow ruled paper), always several lines tall.
